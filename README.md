### 2. Renewable Forecasting at Scale
> **Production ML - NewGrid Consulting LLC**

Operational 7-day (168-hour) power forecasting platform for ~450 solar and wind plants in the ERCOT renewable energy market. ### 3. Machine Unlearning for Recommenders
> **B.Tech Thesis - Heritage Institute of Technology**

Empirical study of privacy vs. performance in recommendation-system unlearning. Compared exact SISA-style retraining with approximate Gradient Ascent and Gradient Difference approaches across Matrix Factorization and transformer-based SASRec on MovieLens-1M. Evaluated beyond accuracy using Membership Inference Attack (MIA) AUC for privacy, Jaccard@10 similarity and recommendation drift for user experience, and computational overhead for deployment feasibility. Key finding: approximate unlearning (Gradient Ascent + SASRec) is the most viable solution for large-scale deployment - lowest MIA AUC, minimal overhead, and high recommendation stability.
